# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- **Plan for someone else:** the new "Plan for" picker plans for your target or a party member (from the game), anyone by name and realm (from the Raider.IO addon's data, when it's installed), or runs you type in.
- **Right-click a player** and pick *Plan their Mythic+ push* to open the planner for them.
- **Where top parses come from:** each planned run shows the key levels the top Warcraft Logs parses of your spec come from in that dungeon ("top parses +14 to +18"). Hover the run for the median key and DPS.
