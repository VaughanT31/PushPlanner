# Changelog

## 1.5.4

## 1.5.3

- **Fresh cutoffs:** today's top 1%, top 0.1%, 10%, 25% and 40% ratings per region from Raider.IO, so the *TOP 1%* box shows how far you are from the line as it stands now.

## 1.5.2

## 1.5.1

- **Fresh numbers:** this season's top 1% and top 0.1% ratings per region from Raider.IO, and the key levels and output top players reach in each dungeon, read again from Warcraft Logs.

## 1.5.0

- **Your group's keys:** in a group, a *Group keys* strip shows the keystone each member holds. Hover one to see what timing it would add to the rating of whoever you're planning for. Group members who run Push Planner share their keys with each other automatically, and so do players running an addon that includes LibKeystone, such as BigWigs, when you have one too. Neither is required.
- **How close to the top 1%:** a new *TOP 1%* box shows this season's top 1% rating for your region and how many points you still need. Hover it for the top 0.1%, 10% and 25% too. The numbers come from Raider.IO and are updated with each release.
- **A wider window:** long dungeon names and run details no longer run under the icons, and there's room for the new boxes.

## 1.4.0

- **A cleaner window:** the settings sit in one panel with Calculate at the end of the row, and the plan has its own framed panel with a heading (*Ways to reach 3600*).
- **Plans straight away:** opening the window shows your plan at once, so there's no empty space waiting for Calculate. Change a setting and press Calculate to plan again.
- **Progress bar:** how far your rating is towards the target, as a bar and a percentage.
- **Colour at a glance:** Current takes your rating's colour, Target is gold, and Gap turns green once you're there.
- **Fixed:** some boxes, dropdowns and checkboxes were missing a side of their border at some UI scales. Borders are now always one full screen pixel.
- Proper tick marks in checkboxes and dropdown lists, which also highlight the row under your mouse.
- **Typed-in runs start from your own season bests,** so a what-if only needs the dungeons you change. The plan underneath is hidden while you edit.
- **Fixed:** pressing Done on the typed-in runs with nothing filled in opened the panel straight back up.

## 1.3.1

- **Top parse bands are filled in:** each planned run now shows the key levels the top Warcraft Logs parses of your spec come from in that dungeon ("top parses +21 to +22"). Hover the run for the median key and DPS. Updated for the current season.

## 1.3.0

- **Plan for someone else:** the new "Plan for" picker plans for your target or a party member (from the game), anyone by name and realm (from the Raider.IO addon's data, when it's installed), or runs you type in.
- **Right-click a player** and pick *Plan their Mythic+ push* to open the planner for them.
- **Where top parses come from:** each planned run shows the key levels the top Warcraft Logs parses of your spec come from in that dungeon ("top parses +14 to +18"). Hover the run for the median key and DPS.
