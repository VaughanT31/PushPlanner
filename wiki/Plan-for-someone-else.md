# Plan for someone else

*Plan a push for your target, a party member, anyone by name, or numbers you type in.*

The **Plan for** picker at the top of the window chooses whose rating to plan for:

- **Me:** read live from your character.
- **Your target or a party member:** from the Mythic+ summary the game shares for players in your group or that you target.
- **Anyone by name** (`Name-Realm`): from the Raider.IO addon's own data, when you have it installed. That data can be a few days old.
- **Typed in:** each dungeon's best key level and whether it was timed, by hand.

### Right-click a player

Right-click a player (their unit frame, their name in chat, your friends list or guild) and choose **Plan their Mythic+ push** to open the planner for them straight away.
