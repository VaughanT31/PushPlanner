# Group keys and the top 1%

*See which key in your group moves you furthest, and how close you are to the top 1%.*

### Your group's keys

In a group, the **Group keys** strip shows the keystone each member holds. Hover a key to see how much rating timing it would add for whoever you're planning for, so you can pick the key that helps most before anyone opens their bags.

- Group members running Push Planner share their keys automatically.
- So do players running an addon that includes LibKeystone, such as BigWigs, when you have one too.
- Neither is required. Without them you see your own key and the keys the game shares.

### How close to the top 1%

The **Top 1%** box shows this season's top 1% rating for your region, from Raider.IO, and how many points you still need. Hover it for the top 0.1%, 10% and 25% cutoffs too.
