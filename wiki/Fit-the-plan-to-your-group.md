# Fit the plan to your group

*Avoid dungeons, cap the key level and keep your inputs between sessions.*

Open the settings with `/pushplanner config` or by right-clicking the minimap button.

- **Avoid dungeons** you don't want to run. The plan routes around them.
- **Cap the key level** at the highest your group is happy to run, so plans stay realistic.
- **Remember your inputs** between sessions, or clear them with `/pushplanner reset`.
- **Minimap button:** left-click opens the planner, right-click opens the settings.
