# Top parses

*Which key levels the best logs of your spec come from, for each dungeon.*

Want better parses, not just rating? Each planned run shows the key levels the top Warcraft Logs parses of your spec come from in that dungeon, for example **top parses +14 to +18**.

Hover it for the median key level and the DPS behind it.

The numbers come with each Push Planner release: small summaries per spec and dungeon, with no player names. Keep the addon updated to keep them current.
