# Push Planner wiki

How to use Push Planner: set a Mythic+ rating, read the plan, plan for a friend, use your group's keys and fit the plan to your group.

Also on the website: [Push Planner wiki](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/wiki/push-planner/) | Get it on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/push-planner)

## Pages

- [Getting started](Getting-started.md): Install it, open it, type the rating you want and press Calculate.
- [Reading the plan](Reading-the-plan.md): What each option card and each run in it tells you.
- [Plan for someone else](Plan-for-someone-else.md): Plan a push for your target, a party member, anyone by name, or numbers you type in.
- [Group keys and the top 1%](Group-keys-and-the-top-1.md): See which key in your group moves you furthest, and how close you are to the top 1%.
- [Top parses](Top-parses.md): Which key levels the best logs of your spec come from, for each dungeon.
- [Fit the plan to your group](Fit-the-plan-to-your-group.md): Avoid dungeons, cap the key level and keep your inputs between sessions.
- [Commands](Commands.md): Every Push Planner chat command.
- [Questions and fixes](Questions-and-fixes.md): Answers to the things people ask most.
