# Getting started

*Install it, open it, type the rating you want and press Calculate.*

Push Planner works out the fewest Mythic+ runs that take you to a rating you pick. It reads your current rating, your best run in every dungeon and this week's affixes straight from your character, so there's nothing to look up or type in.

### Install

1. **CurseForge app:** search for **Push Planner** and click Install.
2. **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/push-planner) and put the folder in `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns`.
3. Start the game, or type `/reload`.

### Your first plan

1. Type `/pushplanner` or left-click the Push Planner button on the minimap.
2. Type the **rating you want** in the target box.
3. Press **Calculate**.
4. Read the option cards, from **Option 1 (Fastest)** to the **Easiest**. See [Reading the plan](Reading-the-plan.md).
