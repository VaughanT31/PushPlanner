# Commands

*Every Push Planner chat command.*

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/pushplanner` | Open or close the planner (`/pp` too, unless another addon took it) |
| `/pushplanner config` | Open the settings |
| `/pushplanner reset` | Clear your target rating, max key level and avoided dungeons |
