Pick a Mythic+ rating. Push Planner shows the fewest keys that get you there. It reads your rating, your best run in every dungeon and this week's affixes straight from your character, then lays out the runs to reach your goal, ranked from fastest to easiest.

  • Exact runs to your target: dungeon, key level and the time you need to beat.
  • Fastest to easiest: choose fewer high keys or more lower ones.
  • Nothing to type: no website, no login, no name and realm. Set a target and go.

How It Works
Because it runs inside the game as your character, Push Planner already knows your current rating, your best score and time in every dungeon, the par timers, the season's dungeons and the current affixes. Enter the rating you want and press Calculate. It works out the smallest sets of runs that get you there and shows each one as an option card, from Option 1 (Fastest) to the Easiest.

Every Run in Detail
Each suggested run shows the dungeon, the key level, the affixes, the time limit and the time you need to beat, plus how much rating it adds.

Plan Around Your Group

  • Avoid dungeons you don't want to run, and the plan routes around them.
  • Cap the key level at the highest your group is happy to run, so plans stay realistic.
  • Remember your inputs between sessions, or reset them in one click.

Light and Standalone
A custom interface with no dependencies and no embedded libraries. A minimap button opens the planner with a left-click and settings with a right-click.

Commands

  • /pp or /pushplanner: open or close the planner
  • /pp config: open settings
  • /pp reset: clear your target rating, max level and avoided dungeons

Requirements
World of Warcraft: Midnight, patch 12.1 (Season 2). No other addons needed.
